# Privacy Policy for Untubed

**Last updated:** April 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Untubed** browser extension (“Untubed”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the extension.

Untubed is developed by **Nieuware** (or replace with your legal name / entity). If you have questions about this policy, contact: **[your contact email or support URL]**.

---

## Summary

Untubed is designed to **filter videos on YouTube’s Subscriptions page** based on rules you configure. The extension **does not sell your data**, **does not show ads**, and **does not require you to create an account** to use its core features.

---

## What data Untubed processes

### Settings and rules you enter

Untubed stores the configuration you provide, including:

- Channel-based filtering rules (for example, channel names or identifiers)
- Optional title-matching keywords and rule modes
- Category groupings and which categories or rules are enabled
- Your **show / hide** preference for filtered videos on the Subscriptions page
- Data you **import** via the extension’s import feature (treated as part of your settings)

This information is stored using **Chrome’s extension storage APIs** (`chrome.storage`), which keeps data **on your device** as part of your browser profile.

### If you use Chrome Sync

If you are signed into Chrome and have **Chrome Sync** enabled, Google may **sync extension data** associated with your account according to **Google’s own privacy policies and sync settings**. Untubed does not operate its own cloud database for this data.

---

## How your data is used

Your stored settings are used **only** to:

- Apply your filtering rules while you browse **YouTube** (specifically where the extension is active)
- Restore your settings when you reopen the browser or reload the extension
- Support optional **export/import** of your settings as a file you control

---

## Where Untubed runs

Untubed runs **inside your browser** as a standard Chrome extension. To function, it needs permission to interact with **YouTube** pages so it can locate subscription feed items and hide or show them according to your rules.

Untubed **does not** inject code into unrelated websites as part of its core purpose.

---

## Data sharing with third parties

Untubed **does not** transmit your rules or browsing activity to servers operated by the developer for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

**Third-party services you may interact with separately:**

- **Google / YouTube:** When you use YouTube, your use of YouTube is governed by **Google’s policies**, independent of Untubed.
- **Google Chrome / Chrome Sync:** If enabled, sync and browser storage are governed by **Google’s policies**.
- **Optional links in the extension UI:** The extension settings page may include links (for example, a “Buy me a coffee” / support page). If you click those links, you leave the extension page and the destination site’s privacy policy applies.

---

## Data retention and deletion

Your settings remain stored until you **remove them** (for example, by clearing rules/categories in the extension, using “Clear all”, uninstalling the extension, or clearing relevant browser/extension data).

If you uninstall Untubed, stored extension data may be removed depending on your browser behavior and settings.

## Children’s privacy

Untubed is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
